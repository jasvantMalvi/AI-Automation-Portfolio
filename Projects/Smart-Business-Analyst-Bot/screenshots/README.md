# Screenshots

This folder contains visual documentation for the Smart Business Analyst Bot workflow and its reporting outputs.

## Screenshot Files

1. `workflow-overview.png` — n8n workflow showing schedule, sales data collection, processing, AI analysis agents, and report delivery.
2. `sales-data-sheet.png` — Source sales transactions in Google Sheets.
3. `ai-analysis-sheet.png` — AI analysis sheet containing the report date, trends, forecast, risk level, risk summary, and actions.
4. `weekly-email-report.png` — Weekly business analysis email with executive summary and key performance metrics.
5. `email-report-details.png` — Email report sections showing forecast, risk summary, and recommended actions.
6. `business-dashboard.png` — Looker Studio business dashboard with KPI cards and sales charts.

The screenshots illustrate the workflow and sample reporting outputs. They do not establish that every metric, forecast, or recommendation is suitable for live financial decisions without source-data validation.
